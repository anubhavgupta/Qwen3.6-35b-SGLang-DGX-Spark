Full context = 262144 tokens. Measured with MAX_TOTAL_TOKENS=0 so SGLang

| Fraction | Mode            | max_total_num_tokens | full contexts | free after boot |
|---------:|-----------------|---------------------:|--------------:|----------------:|
|     0.50 | DFlash block 8  |            1,225,770 |           4.7 |        ~52 GB   |
|     0.90 | DFlash block 8  |            3,357,023 |          12.8 |         ~7 GB   |
|     0.90 | no spec         |            7,544,800 |          28.8 |        ~11 GB   |
|     0.93 | DFlash block 8  |            3,506,760 |          13.4 |         ~8 GB   |
|     0.93 | no spec         |            7,881,624 |          30.1 |       ~10.5 GB  |
|     0.95 | DFlash block 8  |            3,605,460 |         13.75 |  ~5.4 GB (4.9 GB swapped) |
|     0.95 | no spec         |            8,209,240 |          31.3 |        ~4.5 GB  |

  DFlash costs 22 KB of KV per token (10 target + 12 draft) vs 10 KB
  without a draft, so it fits ~2.25x fewer full contexts at the same
  fraction. Rule of thumb from the two DFlash points: ~2.4 full DFlash
  contexts per +0.1 of fraction (0.8 ~ 10.4, 0.7 ~ 8).
  0.9 leaves only 7-11 GB for the OS, page cache and runtime buffers on
  GB10's shared memory: fine to boot, risky under long-context load.
  Keep <= 0.80 for real use.

Takeaways
  - Block 8 (the draft's trained block_size) is the best all-rounder:
    1.8x no-spec at c=1, 1.6x at c=2, and the best c=2 throughput.
  - Gains are workload-dependent: ~2.3-2.4x on reasoning/code, only
    ~1.15-1.3x on open-ended chat/essay prose.
  - Blocks > 8 raise accept length slightly (4.07-4.27) but the wider
    verify step costs more than it saves: c=1 flat-to-worse (block 12's
    reason number is one noisy sample), c=2 clearly worse (127-139).
  - Blocks < 8 lose across the board; block 4 is slower than no-spec on
    essays.
  - fa4 draft attention works on SM121 but is within noise of flashinfer
    and forces a BF16 draft KV (24 KB/token vs 12 KB), so it is not used.
  - MTP (./start-mtp.sh, in-checkpoint head, EAGLE topk 1) peaks at 3
    steps: 1.3x no-spec at c=1, 1.4x at c=2 -- well behind DFlash block 8
    on reasoning/code (91-104 vs 155-163 tok/s), roughly tied on essay
    prose. More steps raise accept length (2.93 -> 3.86) but each MTP step
    is a sequential draft forward, so 6/8 steps lose speed (8 steps is
    slower than no-spec on chat/essay and at c=2). Use MTP only if DFlash
    is unavailable (e.g. YaRN > 262K context, which DFlash refuses).
  - c=1 differences under ~5% are within run-to-run noise (temp 1.0).
